# Design that doesn't look vibe-coded

Aim for clean, intentional, product-grade (think AllTrails / Linear / Stripe) — never the generic centered-card, purple-gradient AI look.

- **Point of view first.** Pick a specific aesthetic and commit to it; don't average toward defaults.
- **Type.** A real type scale, one strong display face + a clean body face, generous line-height. Never leave the default system-font look.
- **Color.** A small semantic palette via tokens (no raw hex scattered in components). One confident accent. Proper light AND dark.
- **Space.** 4/8px rhythm, whitespace as a feature, everything aligned to a grid.
- **Motion.** Subtle and purposeful (150–250ms), and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The details that sell it.** Real content (no lorem), designed empty/loading/error states, hover+focus states, mobile-first responsive.
- **Go deeper when it matters.** For serious design work, invoke the `frontend-design`, `bencium-innovative-ux-designer`, or `ui-ux-pro-max` skills.
- **Verify.** Screenshot in the browser and compare before claiming it's done.
